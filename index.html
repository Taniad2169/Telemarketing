Virtual Receptionist · The 30-Second Cold Outreach Script (26 sec / 71 words)

"Hi [Business Owner / Doctor / Attorney], this is [Caller Name] with The WebSwan. Most practices lose up to 60% of new inquiries simply because they can't answer while with clients—and those callers immediately dial a competitor. We set up dedicated, 24/7 multilingual receptionists trained specifically on your business to answer questions, capture leads, and book appointments day or night—giving you complete peace of mind. Can I email you a quick 2-minute demo of how she answers for your office?"

Recommended Rebuttal Handling

Objection: "We already have a front desk receptionist."
"She isn't meant to replace your staff—she covers the gaps when your lines are busy, during lunch, and after 5:00 PM so zero callers ever hit voicemail."

Objection: "Is this an annoying automated robot?"
"Not at all. She speaks naturally, handles English and Spanish seamlessly, knows your business FAQs, and sounds like a live team member. I can have her call your test line right now so you can hear her for yourself."

Objection: "How much does it cost?"
"While traditional answering services charge $400 to $1,000 a month with strict minute caps, our fully managed 24/7 receptionist is a flat $199 to $299 a month—saving just one missed client covers the entire year."

If They Ask: "What Is ADA / Privacy Compliance?"

"Every state has its own regulations for a business's online presence — your website is legally treated as an extension of your practice, so it needs to be in compliance with the rules where you're licensed. Penalties can be issued without advance warning. When our audit tool flags a site, that same finding can be reported to the state attorney's office — so we always send you the direct link to verify it yourself when we send the audit over. What's the best email, and who should we send it to?"

Rebuttal: "That Won't Happen To Us"

"These penalties are real — there have already been lawsuits in the state of ________, and you can verify that yourself on Google right now. Sephora was fined $1.2 million for website compliance violations — just search “Sephora website compliance penalty” and you'll see it. Think about it this way — people wake up in the morning, get ready for work, and get in their car, and some of them end up in a car accident that day — but nobody plans on getting into an accident, right?" (pause and let them answer) "It's the same with these penalties — they come with zero warning. You could walk into your office one day and find a notice sitting right there in your mailbox. Better to be safe than sorry."
