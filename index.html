Virtual Receptionist · The 30-Second Cold Outreach Script (26 sec / 71 words)

"Hi [Business Owner / Doctor / Attorney], this is [Caller Name] with WebSwan Digital Solutions. Most practices lose up to 60% of new inquiries simply because they can't answer while with clients—and those callers immediately dial a competitor. We set up dedicated, 24/7 multilingual receptionists trained specifically on your business to answer questions, capture leads, and book appointments day or night—giving you complete peace of mind. Can I email you a quick 2-minute demo of how she answers for your office?"

Recommended Rebuttal Handling

Objection: "We already have a front desk receptionist."
"She isn't meant to replace your staff—she covers the gaps when your lines are busy, during lunch, and after 5:00 PM so zero callers ever hit voicemail."

Objection: "Is this an annoying automated robot?"
"Not at all. She speaks naturally, handles English and Spanish seamlessly, knows your business FAQs, and sounds like a live team member. I can have her call your test line right now so you can hear her for yourself."

Objection: "How much does it cost?"
"While traditional answering services charge $400 to $1,000 a month with strict minute caps, our fully managed 24/7 receptionist is a flat $199 to $299 a month—saving just one missed client covers the entire year."

WebSwan Media Network · Online Advertising for Doctors & Attorneys · $289/year (about $24/month)

Sell it as a featured visibility spot plus a direct button to their own client portal — not as “ads.” About 45 seconds:

"Hi [Doctor's Name / Attorney Name], this is [Caller Name] with WebSwan Media Network. I'm reaching out to a select group of [doctors / attorneys] in [area] because we're opening a limited number of featured spots on our online advertising platform.

Here's what makes it different. Your listing isn't just a banner — it includes a button that takes your clients straight to your own secure portal, whether that's [athenahealth / Clio / your client portal or intake form]. So when someone finds you, they go directly to the right place to reach you. Nothing changes on your end, and their private information stays inside your own system.

It's $289 for the entire year — about $24 a month, less than a dollar a day. One new client from being visible pays for that many times over.

Can I send you a quick preview of what your spot would look like?"

Recommended Rebuttal Handling

Objection: "We already advertise."
"This isn't instead of that—it's an added spot that costs about $24 a month and sends people straight to your portal. Think of it as one more door into your practice."

Objection: "How do I know it will work?"
"I can't promise you a specific number of clients—nobody honest can. What you get is visibility plus a direct portal link for about $24 a month. What's one new client worth to your practice?"

Objection: "Is it secure? What about client/patient privacy?"
"Nothing sensitive ever goes through our platform. The button simply opens your own portal, so all client or patient information stays inside the secure system you already use."

Objection: "That's too much / I need to think about it."
"Completely understand. It's $289 for the year, under $0.80 a day. Let me email you the preview so you can see exactly what your spot looks like, and then you can decide. What's the best email?"

Objection: "Send me something in writing."
"Happy to. I'll send a one-page preview with your featured spot and the portal button. Who should I address it to, and what's the best email?"

Caller Reminders

  • Never promise leads, rankings, or specific results. Sell visibility and the direct portal link.
  • Attorney advertising is regulated by each state bar — don't call anything “bar-approved.”
  • Don't claim any portal is HIPAA-compliant or secure unless it's their own system and they say so.
  • Goal of the call: a small yes — permission to send the preview and a confirmed email. Log it as Wants an Email.

If They Ask: "What Is ADA / Privacy Compliance?"

"Every state has its own regulations for a business's online presence — your website is legally treated as an extension of your practice, so it needs to be in compliance with the rules where you're licensed. Penalties can be issued without advance warning. When our audit tool flags a site, that same finding can be reported to the state attorney's office — so we always send you the direct link to verify it yourself when we send the audit over. What's the best email, and who should we send it to?"

Rebuttal: "That Won't Happen To Us"

"These penalties are real — there have already been lawsuits in the state of ________, and you can verify that yourself on Google right now. Sephora was fined $1.2 million for website compliance violations — just search “Sephora website compliance penalty” and you'll see it. Think about it this way — people wake up in the morning, get ready for work, and get in their car, and some of them end up in a car accident that day — but nobody plans on getting into an accident, right?" (pause and let them answer) "It's the same with these penalties — they come with zero warning. You could walk into your office one day and find a notice sitting right there in your mailbox. Better to be safe than sorry."
